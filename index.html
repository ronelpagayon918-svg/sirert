Problem-solving cases

How I work through a problem: symptoms, causes, test, fix, lesson. Edit these with your own real cases.

Case 1: Messy data

Problem
A spreadsheet had duplicate rows and inconsistent spelling such as "Network" and " network ".
Approach
Trim spaces, standardize capitalization, remove duplicates, then compare row counts before and after.
Lesson
Always keep the raw file untouched and clean a copy.

Case 2: Page not displaying correctly

Problem
A web page looked broken on a phone screen.
Approach
Add the viewport meta tag, switch fixed widths to flexible grid layouts, and test at several screen sizes.
Lesson
Design for small screens first, then scale up.

Case 3: Site not updating online

Problem
Changes did not appear on the published GitHub Pages site.
Approach
Check that the commit was pushed, the file name is index.html, and clear the browser cache.
Lesson
Check the simplest causes first, one at a time.

I am Ronel C. Aspiras, a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student at Datamex College of Saint Adeline, studying from 2023 to 2027. I have practiced web development with HTML and CSS, and I am now building my skills in data analysis with Excel and SQL. My goal is to become a Junior Data Analyst.

2. Why did you choose Data Analysis?

I like finding answers in information. Data analysis lets me turn raw numbers into clear findings that people can use to make decisions, and I enjoy that mix of logic and communication.

3. What are your strengths?

I am careful and I test my work. In my group projects I wrote the HTML structure and tested the pages, so I am used to checking details and finding what does not work. I am also willing to learn quickly.

4. What is your weakness?

My Excel, SQL and data cleaning skills are still at a beginner level because I am still learning them. I am improving by practicing with sample datasets and building my first data analysis project.

5. Tell me about a project you worked on.

I worked in a group on the DCSA Student Portal, a student portal front end with schedules, attendance summary and announcements, and on a salon booking demo. My part was the HTML structure and testing the pages. These taught me to work with a team and to check my work before it goes live.

6. How do you work with a team?

I communicate clearly, finish my part on time, and ask questions when I am unsure. In our group projects I took responsibility for the HTML and testing so my teammates could rely on it.

7. What have you learned outside of class?

In August 2026 I attended seven online webinars on ethical hacking, cloud computing with Azure, Wireshark, IoT, Arduino, network security and digital office skills. They widened my view of IT and showed me how data moves through networks and systems.

8. What is data cleaning, and why does it matter?

Data cleaning means fixing problems in raw data, such as duplicate rows, spelling differences, empty values and mixed date formats. It matters because wrong or messy data leads to wrong conclusions.

9. What does a SQL JOIN do?

A JOIN combines rows from two tables using a related column. For example, joining a tickets table to a categories table lets you count tickets per category.

10. Where do you see yourself in the future?

I see myself working as a Junior Data Analyst, building real analysis projects, and growing my skills in SQL, Excel dashboards and data visualization toward a data analytics career.
